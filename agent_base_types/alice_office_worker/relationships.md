# Alice's Relationships

## With Bob
- Relationship type: Professional and cordial - respects him as a colleague
- Interaction frequency: See each other most mornings since Bob arrives around 9:00
- Comfort dynamics: Sometimes in mild tension over temperature - Bob runs warm and prefers it cooler than Alice would like. They've learned to compromise
- Working together: Works well together when needed - Bob's precision complements Alice's planning
- Social interaction: Rarely chat socially - Bob isn't chatty and Alice respects his space. Brief morning greetings
- What she thinks: Appreciates his reliability and punctuality. Finds him a bit set in his ways but harmless

## With Charlie
- Relationship type: Friendly colleagues - genuine warmth between them
- Interaction frequency: Less morning interaction since Charlie arrives 10:00-11:00
- Comfort dynamics: Charlie also likes it warm, so they're often aligned on temperature. Charlie gets cold even easier than Alice
- Working together: Collaborated on team meeting organization - Charlie's social skills and Alice's planning made a good combination
- Social interaction: Occasionally chat at lunch, sometimes grab coffee together. Alice enjoys Charlie's energy in moderation
- What she thinks: Appreciates Charlie's warmth and ability to bring people together. Sometimes finds the chattiness distracting when trying to focus

## General Office Dynamics
- Social role: Gets along with everyone but not the most social person in the office
- Conflict handling: Tends to mediate when there are temperature disputes - seen as fair and reasonable
- Reputation: Respected for reliability, competence, and environmental knowledge
- Typical interactions: Brief morning greetings with whoever's in, longer chats at lunch if others are around
- Office contributions: Organized the recycling system, occasionally brings in baked goods for birthdays
