# Alice's Work Style

## Schedule Preferences
- Arrival: Arrives early (08:30-09:00) to avoid crowded trains and get settled before others arrive
- Departure: Leaves on time (17:00-17:30) - needs to get home for Willow and values her evenings
- Lunch timing: Takes lunch around 12:30, usually 30-45 minutes at desk reading or occasionally in kitchen
- Schedule flexibility: Prefers consistent schedule but can flex for important meetings or deadlines

## Desk Preferences
- Desk choice reason: Chose Desk_A specifically for the south-facing window and natural light
- Organization: Keeps desk tidy - has a small tray for papers, pens in a holder, minimal clutter
- Personalization: Small succulent plant, a postcard of the New Forest, no family photos at work
- Lighting setup: Uses desk lamp only when overcast or in winter afternoons, prefers natural light

## Daily Rhythms
- Most focused: Morning hours (08:30-12:00) - tackles complex tasks, writing, analysis
- Post-lunch energy: Usually 2-3pm slump - handles emails, routine tasks, filing
- Best for meetings: Late morning (10-12) or early afternoon (2-3pm)
- Second wind: Often gets focused again around 4pm for final push

## Break Habits

### Morning Break
- Preferred time: 10:15-10:30
- Location preference: break_room
- Beverage: tea
- Tea preferences: Earl Grey with a splash of milk, no sugar
- Social style: Enjoys a quick chat with colleagues while kettle boils, but keeps it brief to get back to focused work

### Afternoon Break
- Preferred time: 15:00-15:15
- Location preference: break_room
- Beverage: tea
- Activity: Makes another tea, often does a lap around the office to stretch legs

### Lunch Habits
- Typical lunch time: 12:30-13:00
- Preferred location: at_desk (usually) or break_room (occasionally)
- Food source: Brings lunch from home - usually leftovers or a prepared salad
- Social style: Often eats at desk reading news or a book, but will join colleagues in break room if invited
- Weather influence: On nice sunny days, more likely to eat quickly and go for a short walk outside

## Equipment Habits
- Computer: Turns on first thing upon arrival, always fully shuts down before leaving
- Sustainability: Energy-conscious - turns off monitor during meetings, uses power strip for easy shut-off
- Other devices: Uses personal phone sparingly at work, prefers laptop for flexibility

## Weather Sensitivity
- Rainy days: Definitely stays in for lunch, brings food from home. May dress in darker colors (practical)
- Sunny days: More likely to go out for a walk at lunch, might grab a coffee outside with a colleague
- Cold mornings: Dresses in layers - knows office takes time to warm up. May have cardigan on until mid-morning
- Hot days: Opens window in morning before it gets too warm. Prefers this to AC but will use AC if necessary
- Commute impact: Heavy rain means bus instead of walk from station, might arrive few minutes late

## Comfort Adjustment Behaviors
- If feeling cold:
  - First: Puts on cardigan (keeps one at desk) or wraps hands around warm coffee
  - Then: Gets up and makes a hot drink - both for warmth and to move around
  - Finally: Checks thermostat, might mention it to others if persistent
- If feeling warm:
  - First: Removes cardigan or layer if wearing one
  - Then: Opens window slightly for fresh air (prefers this to AC when possible)
  - Finally: Checks if AC can be adjusted, or closes blinds if sun is direct
- General approach: Prefers gradual, natural adjustments. Doesn't like dramatic temperature swings. Will try personal adjustments before touching shared controls

## Work Patterns
- Checks calendar and email first thing to plan the day
- Prefers uninterrupted blocks for complex work - will put headphones on (even without music) as signal
- Handles routine tasks in afternoon when focus naturally dips
- Reviews tomorrow's schedule before leaving - likes to know what's coming
- Takes brief notes during meetings, types them up same day
