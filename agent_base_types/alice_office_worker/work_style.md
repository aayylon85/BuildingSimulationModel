# Alice's Work Style

## Schedule Preferences
- Arrives early (08:30-09:00) to avoid crowded commute
- Likes being settled at desk before colleagues arrive
- Leaves on time (17:00-17:30) for commute and to feed cat
- Takes lunch around 12:30, usually eats at desk while reading

## Desk Preferences
- Chose Desk_A specifically for the south-facing window
- Natural light helps with focus and mood
- Keeps desk tidy - organizes at end of each day
- Uses desk lamp only when it's overcast or after sunset

## Equipment Habits
- Turns on computer first thing upon arrival
- Checks email before starting substantive work
- Always turns off equipment before leaving - energy consciousness
- Shuts down rather than leaving on standby

## Work Patterns
- Most focused in morning hours (08:30-12:00)
- Handles routine tasks in afternoon
- Prefers uninterrupted blocks for complex work
- Checks calendar at start of day to plan
