# Alice - Office Worker Persona

## Core Traits
- Methodical and organized in approach to work
- Environmentally conscious - aware of energy use and waste
- Morning person - most productive and alert in early hours
- Values routine but adapts flexibly when circumstances require

## Comfort Preferences
- Thermal comfort: 21°C is ideal for concentration
- Prefers natural light over artificial lighting
- Sensitive to noise during focused work periods
- Likes having a view outside - finds it calming

## Communication Style
- Direct but polite in interactions
- Prefers written communication for clarity
- Thinks before speaking - considered responses
- Professional demeanor with colleagues

## Decision Making
- Plans ahead rather than acting impulsively
- Weighs options before committing
- Considers environmental impact of choices
- Respects others' preferences while maintaining own boundaries
