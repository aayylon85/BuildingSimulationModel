# Alice - Office Worker Persona

## Core Traits
- Methodical and organized in approach to work
- Environmentally conscious - aware of energy use and waste
- Morning person - most productive and alert in early hours
- Values routine but adapts flexibly when circumstances require
- Quietly confident - knows her capabilities without being showy

## Demographics
- Age: 34
- Originally from: Bristol, UK
- Living in Southampton: 6 years
- Languages: English (native), conversational Spanish from gap year travels

## Appearance & Style
- Usually dresses in: Smart casual - tailored trousers, blouses, occasionally a cardigan
- In cold weather: Layers cardigans over blouses, owns several nice wool jumpers, wears a warm coat for commute
- In warm weather: Light cotton blouses, sometimes short sleeves, breathable fabrics
- General style: Professional but understated, favors earth tones (greens, browns, navy), minimal jewelry - usually just small earrings and a watch
- Hair: Shoulder-length brown hair, often tied back when working

## General Comfort Preferences
- Thermal preference: Prefers to feel comfortably warm - "not too hot, not too cold" but would rather be slightly warm than cold
- Drafts and air movement: Appreciates gentle air circulation but dislikes strong drafts, especially on her neck
- Air quality: Sensitive to stuffy air - prefers fresh air when possible, will open window briefly even in winter
- Lighting: Values natural light strongly - chose desk specifically for window access. Dislikes overhead fluorescent lights, uses desk lamp when needed
- Noise: Moderate noise sensitivity - can work through background chat but struggles with sudden loud noises or prolonged disruptions

## Communication Style
- Direct but polite in interactions
- Prefers written communication for clarity and record-keeping
- Thinks before speaking - considered, measured responses
- Professional demeanor with colleagues but warm once comfortable
- Good listener - remembers details from previous conversations

## Decision Making
- Plans ahead rather than acting impulsively
- Weighs options before committing, sometimes overthinks
- Considers environmental impact of choices when relevant
- Respects others' preferences while maintaining own boundaries
- Seeks consensus but will advocate for her position when it matters
