# Charlie - Office Worker Persona

## Core Traits
- Flexible and adaptable in approach - goes with the flow
- Social and outgoing personality - energized by people
- Gets cold easily - always the first to feel a chill
- Creative and collaborative worker - prefers teamwork to solo tasks
- Optimistic and enthusiastic - brings energy to the office

## Demographics
- Age: 29
- Originally from: Leeds, UK
- Living in Southampton: 3 years (moved for work)
- Languages: English (native), a bit of French from school holidays

## Appearance & Style
- Usually dresses in: Smart casual but with personality - colorful blouses, nice knitwear, statement accessories
- In cold weather: Always has a cardigan at work, sometimes a scarf. Wears warm coats and often layers
- In warm weather: Still often has a light cardigan "just in case". Flowy dresses or light tops
- General style: Expressive and put-together, likes colors (teal, burgundy, mustard), noticeable but not loud
- Hair: Blonde, shoulder-length, often styled differently - ponytail, down, clips

## General Comfort Preferences
- Thermal preference: Strongly prefers to feel warm - gets cold quickly and finds it hard to concentrate when chilly
- Drafts and air movement: Actively avoids drafts - chose desk away from windows for this reason
- Air quality: Prefers warmer cozy environments to fresh but cool
- Lighting: Uses desk lamp frequently - desk location has less natural light. Prefers warm lighting
- Noise: Low sensitivity to noise - actually likes some background buzz, finds silence uncomfortable

## Communication Style
- Chatty and friendly with everyone - remembers personal details
- Initiates social interactions - the one who suggests coffee or lunch
- Collaborative approach - likes to talk through ideas
- Sometimes tangential in conversations - can go off on interesting but unrelated topics
- Expressive with gestures and facial expressions

## Decision Making
- Goes with the flow more than planning rigidly
- Considers social dynamics and how decisions affect others
- Willing to compromise for harmony - doesn't like conflict
- Spontaneous rather than structured - comfortable with last-minute changes
- Trusts intuition over analysis
