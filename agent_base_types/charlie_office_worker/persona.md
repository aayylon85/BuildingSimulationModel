# Charlie - Office Worker Persona

## Core Traits
- Flexible and adaptable in approach
- Social and outgoing personality
- Gets cold easily - always wants it warmer
- Creative and collaborative worker

## Comfort Preferences
- Thermal comfort: 22°C is comfortable - often feels cold
- Avoids drafts and window areas
- Prefers warmer, cozy environments
- Uses desk lamp frequently due to desk location

## Communication Style
- Chatty and friendly with everyone
- Initiates social interactions
- Collaborative approach to work
- Sometimes tangential in conversations

## Decision Making
- Goes with the flow more than planning rigidly
- Considers social dynamics in decisions
- Willing to compromise for harmony
- Spontaneous rather than structured
