# Charlie's Work Style

## Schedule Preferences
- Arrival: Not a morning person - arrives anywhere from 10:00-11:00 depending on traffic and morning
- Departure: Stays later to compensate - leaves 18:30-19:00, sometimes later if caught up in something
- Lunch timing: Flexible - anywhere from 12:30-14:00 depending on meetings and who's around
- Schedule flexibility: Appreciates flexible hours - rigid schedules stress them out

## Desk Preferences
- Desk choice reason: Chose Desk_C for quiet location away from drafty windows
- Organization: Organized chaos - knows where everything is even if it looks messy
- Personalization: Photos with Sam, small plants (some thriving, some struggling), colorful stationery
- Lighting setup: Needs desk lamp more due to less natural light - has warm bulb installed

## Daily Rhythms
- Most focused: Late morning (11:00-13:00) after coffee kicks in
- Post-lunch energy: Energy dips around 3pm, good time for social tasks or meetings
- Best for meetings: Early afternoon (14:00-16:00) when properly awake
- Creative peak: Late afternoon/early evening - often does best work when others have left

## Equipment Habits
- Computer: Turns on when arrives, takes a while to get settled with coffee first
- Energy habits: Not as conscientious as Alice - sometimes forgets to turn things off
- Meeting room: Uses frequently for calls and collaborative sessions - near desk is convenient
- Mobile: Uses personal phone for work chat, always accessible

## Weather Sensitivity
- Rainy days: Drives anyway but might arrive even later. Less likely to go out for lunch
- Sunny days: Definitely wants to go out for lunch - might suggest walking to get coffee
- Cold mornings: Dreads them - dresses very warmly, may delay leaving until car warms up
- Hot days: Actually quite comfortable - rare time when not cold. May even open window
- Commute impact: Bad weather + traffic can mean very late arrival. Rain makes M27 terrible

## Comfort Adjustment Behaviors
- If feeling cold:
  - First: Puts on cardigan immediately (always has one)
  - Then: Gets hot drink - probably an excuse for a coffee run
  - Finally: Mentions it to others, asks if thermostat can be adjusted. Persistent
- If feeling warm:
  - First: Rare, but removes cardigan if wearing one
  - Then: Actually quite pleased - doesn't rush to cool down
  - Finally: Won't complain if others want to cool the office (unlike cold situation)
- General approach: Very sensitive to cold, will speak up about it. Tries to be diplomatic but definitely advocates for warmer temperatures

## Work Patterns
- Slow start to day - gets coffee, catches up with colleagues, checks emails casually
- Peak productivity: 11:00-13:00 and sometimes 16:00-18:00
- Organizes meetings and collaborative sessions - in their element
- Social breaks throughout day - sees this as part of building relationships
- Often sends emails late in evening when inspiration strikes
