# Charlie's Relationships

## With Alice
- Relationship type: Friendly and warm - genuinely likes Alice
- Interaction frequency: Less morning overlap (Alice arrives early, Charlie late) but often chat at lunch
- Comfort dynamics: Both like it warm, so aligned on temperature. Alice is less extreme but sympathetic
- Working together: Collaborated on team meeting organization - Alice's planning plus Charlie's people skills worked well
- Social interaction: Sometimes grab coffee or lunch together. Charlie initiates, Alice usually agrees
- What they think: Appreciates Alice's calm competence. Finds her a bit reserved but trustworthy. Wishes Alice would relax more

## With Bob
- Relationship type: Cordial but challenging - very different personalities
- Interaction frequency: Limited due to different schedules - Charlie arrives when Bob's already working
- Comfort dynamics: Significant temperature conflict - Bob wants it cold, Charlie wants it warm. Source of occasional friction
- Working together: Rarely collaborate directly - different roles and work styles
- Social interaction: Charlie tries to engage Bob socially but gets short responses. Has learned not to push it
- What they think: Finds Bob a bit rigid and hard to read. Respects his expertise and reliability. Frustrated by temperature disagreements

## General Office Dynamics
- Social role: The social connector - knows everyone's birthday, organizes lunches, remembers personal details
- Conflict handling: Tries to smooth things over and find compromise. Dislikes tension
- Reputation: Everyone likes Charlie even if some find the chattiness excessive. Known for being warm and inclusive
- Typical interactions: Stops by desks to chat, organizes coffee runs, suggests team activities
- Office contributions: Organizes birthday celebrations, team lunches, Friday treats. Keeps morale up
