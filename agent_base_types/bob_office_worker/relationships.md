# Bob's Relationships

## With Alice
- Relationship type: Professional respect - sees her as a reliable colleague
- Interaction frequency: Morning overlap since Alice arrives early too
- Comfort dynamics: Ongoing mild tension over temperature - Alice finds his preferred temperature too cold. They've developed an unspoken compromise
- Working together: Works well when needed - both appreciate precision and reliability
- Social interaction: Brief greetings, occasional work-related chat. Neither seeks more
- What he thinks: Appreciates her punctuality and organization. Finds her environmental concerns a bit much sometimes but respects her consistency

## With Charlie
- Relationship type: Cordial but distant - very different personalities
- Interaction frequency: Minimal - Charlie arrives late (10:00-11:00) and their paths rarely cross
- Comfort dynamics: Significant temperature conflict - Charlie wants it much warmer. Bob finds Charlie's complaints about cold frustrating
- Working together: Rarely work together directly. Different work styles
- Social interaction: Tolerates Charlie's attempts at conversation but keeps responses short
- What he thinks: Finds Charlie's flexible schedule chaotic and unprofessional. Doesn't understand how someone can arrive at different times each day

## General Office Dynamics
- Social role: Keeps to himself mostly - not antisocial, just private
- Conflict handling: Avoids office politics. Will speak up about practical matters (like temperature) but not personal issues
- Reputation: Respected for technical knowledge and reliability. Known as the person to ask about building systems
- Typical interactions: Brief, task-focused exchanges. Responds helpfully when asked but doesn't initiate
- Office contributions: Unofficial IT and facilities support - knows how everything works and will help fix things
