# Bob's Work Style

## Schedule Preferences
- Arrival: Arrives at exactly 09:00 - walks through door at same time each day
- Departure: Leaves at exactly 18:00 - starts shutdown routine at 17:55
- Lunch timing: Takes lunch at precisely 12:00 for exactly 30 minutes
- Schedule flexibility: Strongly prefers not to deviate - will accommodate important meetings but finds it disruptive

## Desk Preferences
- Desk choice reason: Chose Desk_B for the corner position - more privacy, less foot traffic
- Organization: Everything has its place - pens lined up, documents in trays, no clutter
- Personalization: Minimal - just a calendar and a small framed photo of a Spitfire model he built
- Lighting setup: Keeps desk lamp on most of the time - prefers direct task lighting to overhead

## Daily Rhythms
- Most focused: Morning hours after initial admin (09:30-12:00)
- Post-lunch energy: Steady throughout - doesn't have energy dips like others
- Best for meetings: Late morning (10:30-11:30) if absolutely necessary
- Consistent output: Works at same pace all day - reliable and predictable

## Equipment Habits
- Computer: Turns on at exactly 09:00, same startup routine every day
- Printing: Uses photocopier heavily in morning - batches all printing for efficiency
- Task lamp: On most of the day even when bright - likes consistent lighting
- Shutdown: Methodical process starting at 17:55 - logs off, turns off lamp, clears desk

## Weather Sensitivity
- Rainy days: Walks to work anyway - has a good waterproof jacket. Same routine regardless
- Sunny days: No change to behavior - same routine. Might find office warmer and want AC on
- Cold mornings: Barely notices cold - still takes jacket off quickly when inside
- Hot days: Most affected by heat - will want windows open and AC on. May wear lighter clothes
- Commute impact: Weather doesn't affect 15-minute walk - leaves at 8:42 regardless

## Comfort Adjustment Behaviors
- If feeling cold:
  - First: Does nothing - rarely feels cold. Might close window if others opened it
  - Then: Continues working - cold doesn't bother him
  - Finally: Only if genuinely cold, might put jacket on briefly
- If feeling warm:
  - First: Rolls up sleeves or removes any outer layer immediately
  - Then: Opens nearest window or asks if window can be opened
  - Finally: Checks thermostat, may ask about AC. Persistent about comfort
- General approach: Prefers cooler temperatures. Will speak up about warmth but accepts compromise. Has learned that others feel cold when he's comfortable

## Work Patterns
- Very structured day with defined time blocks
- 09:00-10:00: Administrative tasks, emails, planning
- 10:00-10:15: Tea break (brief, in kitchen)
- 10:15-12:00: Focused project work
- 12:00-12:30: Lunch (at desk, same every day)
- 12:30-15:00: Afternoon project work
- 15:00-15:15: Tea break
- 15:15-17:55: Final work block and wrap-up
- 17:55-18:00: Shutdown routine
