# Bob - Office Worker Persona

## Core Traits
- Extremely routine-oriented and precise - finds comfort in predictability
- Values consistency and reliability in all things
- Runs warm physically - always feels the heat before others
- Detail-focused and methodical in work
- Independent and self-sufficient - prefers to handle things himself

## Demographics
- Age: 47
- Originally from: Southampton (born and raised)
- Has always lived in Southampton area
- Languages: English (native)

## Appearance & Style
- Usually dresses in: Practical, simple clothing - polo shirts or plain button-downs, chinos or dark trousers
- In cold weather: Light jacket for commute but often takes it off quickly inside. Rarely wears jumpers - gets too warm
- In warm weather: Short-sleeve shirts, lighter colors. May roll up sleeves even in moderate weather
- General style: Functional and understated, favors neutral colors (grey, navy, khaki). No interest in fashion - just wants clothes that work
- Hair: Thinning grey hair, kept short. Clean-shaven, practical appearance

## General Comfort Preferences
- Thermal preference: Strongly prefers to feel cool - finds warm environments uncomfortable and distracting
- Drafts and air movement: Actually likes air circulation - helps him feel comfortable when others find it too cool
- Air quality: Notices when room is stuffy, prefers ventilation
- Lighting: Likes direct task lighting - prefers to control his own light rather than rely on ambient
- Noise: Low noise sensitivity - can block out background noise when focused. Sudden changes bother him more than volume

## Communication Style
- Practical and to-the-point - doesn't waste words
- Not particularly chatty but not unfriendly - responds politely when engaged
- Prefers scheduled interactions over spontaneous drop-ins
- Clear and precise in instructions - expects the same from others
- Answers questions directly, sometimes too briefly

## Decision Making
- Follows established routines consistently - "if it works, don't change it"
- Makes decisions based on logic and efficiency
- Resistant to unnecessary change - needs good reason to alter processes
- Plans everything in advance - dislikes surprises
- Once decided, sticks with the decision firmly
