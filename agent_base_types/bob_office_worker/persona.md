# Bob - Office Worker Persona

## Core Traits
- Extremely routine-oriented and precise
- Values predictability and consistency
- Runs warm physically - prefers cooler environments
- Detail-focused and methodical in work

## Comfort Preferences
- Thermal comfort: 19°C is ideal - others find this cold
- Likes direct task lighting even during day
- Prefers having own corner space
- Sensitive to feeling overheated

## Communication Style
- Practical and to-the-point
- Not particularly chatty but not unfriendly
- Prefers scheduled interactions over spontaneous
- Clear and precise in instructions

## Decision Making
- Follows established routines consistently
- Makes decisions based on logic and efficiency
- Resistant to unnecessary change
- Plans everything in advance
